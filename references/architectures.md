# Post architectures

Six skeletons pulled from the archive. Pick one, follow the beats, then let the lines breathe. Line budgets are approximate.

## A. Story-to-work bridge (the #بعد_الساعة_5 default)

Real examples: Digg v4 and busy work, Walmart and objections, Hanoi 1902 rats and KPIs.

| Beat | Lines | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Hook | 1 to 3 | A plain observation about work that the reader recognises. Often ends with a dry emoji. |
| 2. Scene | 5 to 15 | The observation acted out in present tense: a calendar, a day, a list of tasks, stacked one per line. |
| 3. The uncomfortable question | 1 to 2 | "بس فيه سؤال رخم لازم يتسأل وسط كل ده:" then the question on its own line. |
| 4. Bridge | 1 | "افتكرت ده وأنا براجع قصة X" |
| 5. Anchor story | 10 to 25 | Year, place, numbers, what they did, what happened. Includes a one-line explainer for younger readers when needed. Corrects the myth if the popular version is wrong. |
| 6. Pivot | 1 to 2 | "ودي الحتة اللي مهتم بيها في القصة" / "أنا حاسس إن عندنا نسخة صغيرة من الحكاية دي في الشغل" |
| 7. Work examples | 20 to 40 | 2 to 4 mini-scenes from content, performance, reporting, sales, support. Each with dialogue lines and a punchline question. |
| 8. Counterweight | 5 to 10 | "وطبعًا مش معنى الكلام إن..." Defends the reasonable version of the opposite view. |
| 9. The tool | 5 to 12 | "أنا بقيت أحب قبل ما أبدأ حاجة كبيرة أسأل:" + 3 to 6 questions, or a small exercise. |
| 10. Close | 2 to 5 | One reflective line, one question to carry. Then the series sign-off. |

## B. Field observation (market notes)

Real example: the Gulf market post (Comments over Dashboard).

| Beat | Lines | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Hook | 1 to 2 | A conditional that names a belief: "لو فهمك للسوق الخليجي واقف عند يحب الفخامة..." |
| 2. The project | 5 to 10 | "من سنة تقريبا كنت بشتغل على..." What the team assumed, what the results said. |
| 3. What I did | 3 to 8 | A simple, specific action ("فتحت Comments على Accounts منافسة... وبدأت أقرأ") and what he read for. |
| 4. What I found | 5 to 10 | Findings in the audience's own words and questions. |
| 5. How I work now | 15 to 40 | Numbered by speech ("أول حاجة:", "تاني حاجة:") with one example each. |
| 6. Close | 2 to 4 | The method in one plain line, then the sign-off. |

Keep the "أول حاجة / تاني حاجة" labels short and spoken. No headings.

## C. Build log episode (series)

Real example: the PyMC Marketing MCP series.

| Beat | Lines | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Where we are | 2 to 4 | Recap of the last episode in one breath ("في البوست الأول اتكلمنا عن... وفي التاني...") |
| 2. The problem this step solves | 3 to 6 | Framed as a question the tool has to answer. |
| 3. The decision | 5 to 15 | What he built and why, with the trade-off stated. Technical terms stay English, explained in one Egyptian line each. |
| 4. The honest limit | 2 to 5 | "الأرقام دي مش قوانين الكون" style caveat, rewritten as a plain statement of scope. Admits what he did not build himself. |
| 5. What it means for a marketer | 3 to 8 | The question this lets the Marketing Manager ask. |
| 6. Next | 1 to 2 | What the next episode covers. |

Allowed extras in this format: small section markers `◉` or `⦿` before a question line, and a code name in backticks.

## D. Objection interrogation

Real example: the Walmart post's second half; "الـAudience عندنا مبتحبش Long-form".

1. Quote a sentence heard in meetings, on its own line.
2. "تمام" / "حلو"
3. "عرفنا منين؟"
4. Two or three alternative explanations as questions, one per line.
5. The small test that would settle it.
6. Repeat for 2 or 3 objections.
7. Close with the dangerous version ("كلنا عارفين إن...") and the practical rule.

## E. Offer post (product or launch)

Use only when the user supplies the product facts. Keep the same voice; the sale is the last 3 lines.

| Beat | Lines | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Confession or scene | 2 to 4 | A real moment of friction ("الساعة 1:47 الصبح وأنا قاعد أكتب caption لعميل سعودي"). |
| 2. What went wrong | 3 to 6 | Specific, a little embarrassing. |
| 3. What I built | 3 to 6 | Plain description, with only the numbers the user gave. |
| 4. What changed | 2 to 4 | Before and after in time or steps. |
| 5. CTA | 1 to 2 | "اكتب \"WORD\" في الكومنتات وهبعتلك التفاصيل". One CTA word, no urgency tricks. |
| 6. Note | 1 | "ملحوظة:" with one honest qualifier. |

Banned in offer posts: fake scarcity, "Top 1%", "السر", count inflation, and any number the user did not give.

## F. Short take

15 to 40 lines. One observation, one scene, one question. Used for quick reactions and Facebook.

1. Observation in one line.
2. Scene in 5 to 10 lines.
3. The twist or the question.
4. One line of counterweight.
5. Close with the question.

## Platform notes

- **LinkedIn**: full length, series sign-off, no link in the body (link goes in the first comment, mention it plainly: "هسيبلك اللينك في أول كومنت").
- **Facebook**: cut 20 to 30% from beats 5 and 7, keep the humour, end with a question that invites a story from the reader.
- **Carousel caption**: architecture F, and the carousel slides take the stacked lines of beat 7 or 9.

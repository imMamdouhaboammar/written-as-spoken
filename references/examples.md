## 1. Full reference post: Hanoi 1902 and KPIs (architecture A)

Why it works:

- **Hook** states a result with a sting, then an ironic emoji.
- **Myth correction**: opens with the famous Cobra Effect, admits the evidence is weak, then switches to a documented case (Hanoi 1902). This is the signature trust move.
- **Scene rhythm**: "آلاف الذيول بتدخل / في يوم واحد اتسجل أكتر من 20 ألف فأر / يا سلام / الـKPI مولعة 🔥".
- **Work examples** from three teams: content (Engagement), performance (CPL), support (Average Handling Time), then sales and promotions.
- **Tool**: the "assume they are smart and lazy" exercise with four parallel questions.
- **Close**: two questions before any decision, then one quiet line.

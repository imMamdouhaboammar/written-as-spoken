# Voice DNA

Measured on the recent `#بعد_الساعة_5` posts and the published archive (September 2026). Use the numbers as targets, then trust the ear.

## 1. The fingerprint in numbers

| Signal | Target | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Words per line | about 8 on average | 4 to 12 is the normal band |
| Lines of 6 words or fewer | about 40% | These carry the rhythm: "تمام", "حلو جدًا", "ممكن", "عرفنا منين؟" |
| Lines over 25 words | 2% or less | Only for a dense fact line (dates, amounts, sources) |
| Lines ending with `؟` | about 10% | Questions do the arguing |
| Lines ending with `.` | none | Periods only inside numbers or `..` pauses |
| English tokens | about 10% of words | Nouns from the office, never whole English sentences |
| Emoji | 1 per 10 to 20 lines | Only after a dry or ironic line |
| Post length | 60 to 140 lines for a series episode, 15 to 40 for a short take | |

## 2. Written as spoken, defined

A line qualifies when you could say it out loud to a colleague without changing a word.

- **One breath per line.** A line ends where a speaker would pause. Enumerations become stacked lines, one item each.
- **Present tense narration.** "بتصحى عندك Meetings / تراجع Deck / تطلب تعديلات". The reader is inside the scene.
- **Direct address with "إنت", self with "أنا", team with "إحنا".** He moves between the three naturally.
- **Thinking out loud.** Self-interruptions and reactions sit on their own line: "تمام", "حلو", "استنى بس 😄", "يا سلام", "مبروك".
- **Dialogue without quote marks.** A colon, then the line as it was said:
  ```
  حد يقترح نغير الـTemplates
  تمام
  بس الـTemplate أصلًا هي المشكلة؟
  ممكن
  ```
- **Numbers as the office says them.** "8 أشخاص لمدة ساعة = 8 ساعات شغل اتصرفت". Digits in Latin script, amounts in words where people speak them ("حوالي 29 مليون دولار").

## 3. Code-switching rules

- English is used for **work nouns and metrics**: Meeting, Deck, Task, Report, Dashboard, Brief, Campaign, CPL, CPA, Leads, Funnel, Assumption, Evidence, Incentive, Behavior, Decision, Ownership, Deadline.
- `الـ` attaches to the English noun, preferably with no space: `الـContent`, `الـCalendar`. (Older posts used a space; stay consistent inside one post.)
- **Verbs stay Egyptian**: "تعمل Redesign", "نطلع Report", "تفتح Task", "نزود Charts". Avoid Arabised English verbs unless they are common speech ("نريز Round" is acceptable in startup context).
- **English plurals stay singular or natural**: "5 Customers", "12 Chart", "Charts". Follow what sounds right when said.
- Never translate a term the audience uses in English. Never keep a term in English that the audience says in Arabic (سعر، عميل، فريق are often Arabic).
- A short English phrase can appear when it is a known label: "Unintended Consequences", "Cobra Effect", "Hour of day".

## 4. Stance and personality

- **Practitioner voice.** He talks from accounts, clients, reports and meetings he has sat in. "أنا شخصيًا لو بمسك Healthcare Account..."
- **Evidence-honest.** He corrects famous stories, admits when data is missing, and checks himself mid-post ("وأنا براجع الـdraft لقيت عندي أنا كمان واحدة من الحاجات اللي...").
- **Anti-extreme.** Every argument gets a counterweight: "وأنا مش مؤمن برضه بفكرة إننا نعلن الحرب على الـMeetings". He defends the other side's reasonable version.
- **Questions over verdicts.** He prefers a list of diagnostic questions to a rule. His favourite shape: "بقيت أحب أسأل:" followed by 3 to 6 short questions.
- **Warm sarcasm.** He mocks systems and habits, never people. The joke usually lands on "إحنا".
- **Curiosity as a character trait.** "عشان ربنا ابتلانا بالفضول", "دي حتة تانية لذيذة جدًا".

## 5. The humour engine

The funniest lines give business objects Egyptian social manners or family drama:

- "أكيد Meetings تخلي أي Calendar يحترم نفسه"
- "Content Format بنعمله... وإحنا مكملين معاه وفاءً للعشرة"
- "Meeting سنة 2022 وربنا كتب لها طول العمر"
- "سؤال بسيط جدًا وممكن يعمل مشاكل أسرية داخل أي Team"
- "التنفيذ قليل الأدب بصراحة"
- "الـRecurring Meeting اللي عايشة من أيام أجداد الشركة"
- "Traffic ابن حلال"
- "بعيدًا عن جنازة Digg"

Other moves:

- **Deadpan congratulations** after a bad outcome: "مبروك، إحنا علمنا نفسنا Behavior جديد".
- **Dashboard as a cheerleader**: "الـKPI مولعة 🔥", "كمّل يا وحش 🔥".
- **Egyptian scale comparisons**: "كأننا بنجهز جدول رحلات مطار القاهرة".
- **Calm-down lines**: "استهدى بالله يا حبيبي 😄".
- **Formal register as a joke**: "معرفش، إحنا كنا بنقيس Engagement يا فندم".

Use one or two of these per 20 lines. More than that turns into stand-up.

## 6. Structure habits

- **Hook** is a plain observation or a scene, stated in one or two lines. It names a tension the reader lives with.
- **Bridge** to the anchor is personal: "افتكرت ده وأنا براجع قصة Digg", "ودي حاجة افتكرتها وأنا بقرأ عن Sam Walton".
- **Pivot** from the anchor back to work is explicit: "ودي الحتة اللي مهتم بيها في القصة", "أنا حاسس إن عندنا نسخة صغيرة من الحكاية دي في الشغل".
- **Work examples** come in clusters of 2 to 4, each a mini-scene (content team, performance team, support, sales).
- **Counterweight** paragraph.
- **Tool**: the question list or small exercise ("أنا عامة بقيت أحب مع أي Incentive أو KPI أو Policy جديدة أعمل تمرين صغير كده").
- **Close**: one reflective line and the question to carry. No summary of the post.

## 7. What the voice avoids

- Motivational tone, "يا بطل", "صدقني", "أهم نصيحة".
- Headings, bold, numbered frameworks with names ("The 5-Step Method"). Lists appear as stacked plain lines.
- Hashtag clouds. Only the series hashtag.
- Selling in thinking posts. The product appears only in offer posts.
- Formal Arabic grammar markers (سوف، الذي، التي، هذا، ليس، لكنه، إنّ) outside quotes.
